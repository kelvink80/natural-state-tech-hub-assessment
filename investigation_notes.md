# Investigation Notes

## Approach

The investigation was conducted by:

1. Establishing expected reporting frequencies.
2. Identifying reporting gaps.
3. Reviewing battery performance.
4. Correlating telemetry with maintenance records.
5. Distinguishing device failures from gateway failures.
6. Prioritising operational response.

## Key Findings

- SM-03 probable device failure.
- SM-08 prolonged outage following physical disruption.
- SM-05 and SM-07 operating on legacy firmware.
- GW-01 connectivity instability.
- GW-02 restart event.

## Operational Priorities

P1:
- SM-03
- SM-08
- GW-01

P2:
- GW-02

P3:
- Firmware standardisation.
